# Telos at 0%

Select **0%** and enable **Practice mode** while learning. Verify the result and record the instance mode after the fight. For a subsequent normal clear, verify and claim the reward. The published 0% fight has four 100,000 HP phases. The three P4 fonts occur at 75,000, 50,000 and 25,000 remaining HP. [RuneScape Wiki encounter reference](https://runescape.wiki/w/Telos,_the_Warden/Strategies)

For the 0% milestone, use prayer for survival and healing. Leave Rocktails and Saradomin brews unused. Start with full life points and verify the initial protection overhead before waking Telos.

## Visual cues and responses to test

These are guide-derived cues. Confirm the visible phase, targetability and action effect before acting on a cue. Attack patterns can carry across phases, so avoid relying on a timer that restarts at every transition.

| Segment | Cue | Response to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Tendrils bind the player and drain resources | Keep a strong damage ability available and attack through the bind. |
| P1 and later | Charge or uppercut warning | Move off the attack line with space to land, or use an eligible defensive. |
| P1 and later | Hold Still sequence | Use stun protection, survive the heavy melee hit, then move away from the landing area. A preceding ordinary hit can consume a one-hit defensive. |
| P2 | Black beam and charging meter | Pull Telos out of the beam while he can still move. Use magic protection during the magic barrage if needed. |
| P3 | Red beam, magic autos, golems | Use magic protection for sustained autos. Watch the meter and keep Telos out of the beam when repositioning. |
| P4 | Target drops and Telos moves to a font | Heal before entering the active font, protect against melee golems, and prioritize the red golem if its stun threatens the charge. Stay at the font until it intercepts the bomb. Reacquire Telos after the intermission. |
| P4 | Weak anima bomb debuff | Step near a font to cleanse it. Check the overload timer afterwards. |
| P4 | Third font complete | Reacquire Telos for the last damage segment. Verify the encounter outcome after the final hit. |

The guide describes five HP payments at each font and a defensive cooldown after interception. Bring enough health for the final payment and any golem hits. Treat each font as a movement and survival checkpoint. [PVME Telos Basic](https://pvme.io/pvme-guides/basic-guides/telos-basic/)
