# Getting to Telos

## War's Retreat

Use **War's Retreat Teleport** directly. The account owner confirmed that the account has the unlock. At the hub, hover the portal to confirm **Enter Portal (Telos)**. Its attunement and destination at the Heart were verified on 2026-11-13. The bank chest is near the boss portals.

**Verified fallback.** Open the Lodestone Network and choose Draynor Village (`D`). Face the blue Underworld portal and touch **War's Insignia**, the small golden skull on its right. The account owner also confirmed Max Guild portal access. [War's Retreat reference](https://runescape.wiki/w/War%27s_Retreat)

## Starting a run

At the Heart, choose **Jump to platform** on the Roots, select **0% enrage**, and check **Practice mode** before clicking **Start** while learning. At the staging platform, use **Jump to platform** on the Roots again. If the game asks for a number, enter `0` and submit it. The second prompt starts the crossing animation. Wait until the character is standing across the gap on Telos's platform. The reader can see dormant Telos from either side. Capture a new frame after crossing and choose an approach tile beside Telos.

## First-time access on another account

**Guide reference.** The Heart of Gielinor is southwest of Nardah. The four faction generals supply the pieces of the Ancient Sigil, which unlocks the central chamber permanently. The Bandit Camp lodestone is a walking fallback. The route crosses the River Elid bridge before reaching the dungeon. [Heart of Gielinor reference](https://runescape.wiki/w/Heart_of_Gielinor)
