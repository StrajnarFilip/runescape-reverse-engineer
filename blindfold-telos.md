### Context from the video description

- Lucario says the challenge took 46 hours. He used the numeric keypad to
  move the cursor in eight directions, with keypad 5 for a left click. Holding
  Ctrl increased the step to a configurable fixed distance, so he could
  navigate by counting taps from a known cursor position.
- He planned Phases 1–4 to reduce randomness. Missed ticks forced recoveries
  in the first two phases. He later attributed the apparent adrenaline
  problem after Phase 1 to Asphyxiate still being on cooldown.
- He suspected a missed prayer flick during Omnipower in Phase 3. At the first
  font in Phase 4, he says he cast one tick too late after Anticipation and
  missed Entangle. He got the volcanic and corrupt golems and finished Phase 5
  despite being unable to select the Seren godbow special attack stored in
  his Essence of Finality.
