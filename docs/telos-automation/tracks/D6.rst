Goal
----

Be the single writer to the actuator. Collect the intents every producer emits
for a sample, pick what is actually sent within the tick budget, track what is
awaiting confirmation, and drop what has expired.

----------

* ``python/rs3_reader/bot/intents.py`` and ``actuator.py`` from S0a.
* ``docs/prayer-flick.rst``, section "Planner contract" (the acknowledgement
  pattern to generalize).
* ``docs/telos-melee-research.rst`` on queued versus stalled abilities.

* ``tests/python/test_bot_arbiter.py``

Depends on
----------

S0a.

Delivers
--------

* Priority order: survival, then prayer, then mechanics, then rotation.
* Separate budgets for actions on the GCD, actions off the GCD and pointer
  actions.
* No re-send of an unconfirmed intent before its confirmation timeout.
* A decision log line per sample: what was sent, what was deferred, and why.

Done when
---------

Tests with synthetic producers show the order and budgets hold, a
lower-priority intent never delays a higher one, expired intents are dropped,
and a rejected send is reported to the producer.

Needs the user
--------------

Nothing.

Implementation and verification (2026-11-13)
------------------------------------------

``Arbiter.step(WorldState, proposals=None)`` is the serialized single writer.
It can collect registered producers or arbitrate a supplied batch. Survival,
prayer, mechanics and rotation priorities are sorted before dispatch; equal
priority uses the earliest deadline. A low-priority proposal never executes
before a higher-priority eligible proposal in the same batch.

Budgets are per verified server tick: GCD 1, off-GCD 2, food 1, potion 1,
prayer 4 and pointer 3, total 12 by default. They are configurable and distinct
from game cooldown groups. Food, potion, ability and prayer can share a tick.
Unknown ticks do not become wall-clock or client-logic estimates. Attempted
device sends spend budget conservatively even if rejected.

Deadlines, source ages, session/build identity, current preconditions and
producer can_send are checked at dispatch. A latest-world provider can supply
a newer frame. Pointer and prayer input additionally require injected A2/D2
validators; default absence blocks them. Unknown castability, timers, GCD,
prayer state or exact inventory preconditions reject the relevant action.

Pending actions are not resent with new IDs. GCD/food/potion/prayer/pointer
lanes are exclusive while awaiting observation; different off-GCD abilities
have separate pending keys. Newer prayer, ability cooldown, destination tile
or inventory-precondition observations release pending state. A changed slot
is not described as successful consumption. Partial omissions do not prove
absence. Arbitrary interactions require a producer confirm hook returning a
fresh Observation[bool]. Timeout, scene/build/clock changes, callback faults
or uncertain transport results stop and close the backend.

Optional producer hooks: can_send(intent, world), note_sent(id, ns),
note_rejected(id, reason, ns), confirm(intent, before, after). Accepted and
rejected results are also available in the per-step results tuple and an
optional on_result callback. Deferred proposals are logged and recomputed,
not stored as a stale future queue. One compact DecisionLog is returned per
sample and optionally written as JSON Lines; game success is never inferred
from accepted input. History, pending state and proposal batches are bounded.

Twenty D6 tests pass against a fake actuator, including priority, simultaneous
lanes, rejection callbacks, expiry during dispatch, pending confirmation,
fault cleanup, missing validators, changed snapshots, bounded budgets and
S0a replay. The real native-hit capture is replayed with diagnostic synthetic
proposals; unavailable readiness yields no device acceptance. Its golden
proposal-log SHA-256 is
``ac12cf5af97182bb4f31c8999a6b54d2b0429cd46490a6c1993967f9f7c2ac78``.
Raw capture and logs remain local. ``create_stack`` constructs only the
recording fake, with no live producers; no native backend is imported.

Requests
--------

* L: align A1 and D6 budgets/ages, provide a verified server-tick source and
  fresh world provider, and opt in only at an approved ladder rung. Live input
  is disabled by default; the recording-only replay adapter cannot choose a
  hardware backend.
* D2/A2: provide the injected prayer/pointer validators. Prayer validation must
  check the phase/enrage timing scope and coverage; pointer validation must
  check calibrated geometry/visibility. An enabled arbiter alone supplies
  neither permission nor evidence for those actions.
* D3/D4/D5/D7: use note_sent only after acceptance; rejected sends are delivered
  through note_rejected when implemented, plus results/on_result. Implement
  can_send for rules needing revalidation against newer snapshots. Producers
  without a rejection hook must consume the result mailbox through their L
  adapter; no acceptance or success is silently manufactured for them.
* D7/A2: interaction confirmation needs a fresh observable postcondition;
  pointer movement or an open dialog alone does not confirm an arbitrary
  interaction. D8 should stop on arbiter/producer faults and pending timeouts.
