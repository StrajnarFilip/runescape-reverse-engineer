D3 - Survival manager
=====================

:Status: in progress
:Wave: 1
:Branch: ``telos/D3``
:Kind: Python

Shared rules and procedure: ``../README.rst``.

Goal
----

Keep the player alive. Produce intents for food and brews, defensive abilities
on the specific hits they are meant for (Resonance, Freedom, Anticipation,
Devotion, Barricade, Immortality, Disruption Shield), virus cleansing, prayer
restoration and an emergency exit.

Read first
----------

* ``docs/telos-melee-research.rst``, sections "Prayer and defensive decisions",
  "Food, potions and inventory semantics" and "Disruption Shield and
  Vengeance".
* ``docs/inventory-and-abilities.rst`` (item families, doses, cooldown timers).
* ``python/rs3_reader/inventory_records.py``.
* S0a contracts.

Owns
----

* ``python/rs3_reader/bot/survival.py``
* ``profiles/survival.json`` (thresholds and item roles; no code constants)
* ``tests/python/test_bot_survival.py``

Depends on
----------

S0a. Uses P3 effects and D1 threats when present; without them it falls back to
life-point thresholds only and never claims a defensive is correctly timed.

Delivers
--------

Survival intents at the highest priority, each with its trigger as evidence.
Item roles (heal, restore, brew, renewal) come from catalog families, not from
names or slots. Rules taken from guides are tagged as leads in the profile.

Done when
---------

Scenario tests pass for: low life points with and without food, empty
inventory, unknown life points, a predicted heavy hit, a stun, a virus, low
prayer, and the emergency exit when nothing else is available.

Needs the user
--------------

Nothing in this track. Live use starts at rung 3 of track L.

Requests
--------

None yet.
