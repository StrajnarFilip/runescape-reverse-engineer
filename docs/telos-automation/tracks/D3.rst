D3 - Survival manager
=====================

:Status: done
:Wave: 1
:Branch: ``telos/D3``
:Kind: Python

Shared rules and procedure: ``../README.rst``.

Goal
----

Keep the player alive. Produce intents for food and brews, defensive abilities
on the specific hits they are meant for (Resonance, Freedom, Anticipation,
Devotion, Barricade, Immortality, Disruption Shield), virus cleansing, prayer
restoration and an emergency exit.

Read first
----------

* ``docs/telos-melee-research.rst``, sections "Prayer and defensive decisions",
  "Food, potions and inventory semantics" and "Disruption Shield and
  Vengeance".
* ``docs/inventory-and-abilities.rst`` (item families, doses, cooldown timers).
* ``python/rs3_reader/inventory_records.py``.
* S0a contracts.

Owns
----

* ``python/rs3_reader/bot/survival.py``
* ``profiles/survival.json`` (thresholds and item roles; no code constants)
* ``tests/python/test_bot_survival.py``

Depends on
----------

S0a. Uses P3 effects and D1 threats when present; without them it falls back to
life-point thresholds only and never claims a defensive is correctly timed.

Delivers
--------

Survival intents at the highest priority, each with its trigger as evidence.
Item roles (heal, restore, brew, renewal) come from catalog families, not from
names or slots. Rules taken from guides are tagged as leads in the profile.

Done when
---------

Scenario tests pass for: low life points with and without food, empty
inventory, unknown life points, a predicted heavy hit, a stun, a virus, low
prayer, and the emergency exit when nothing else is available.

Needs the user
--------------

Nothing in this track. Live use starts at rung 3 of track L.

Implementation and verification (2026-11-13)
------------------------------------------

``SurvivalManager.step(WorldState, encounter=None)`` emits only S0a intents at
SURVIVAL priority; it acquires no data and never loads an actuator or DLL.
``create_stack()`` exposes the threshold/effect-only producer to S0a replay.
The profile contains all thresholds, item roles, priorities and defensive IDs.
These defaults are policy, not a claim of optimal live rotations.

Food and potion proposals use current native metadata, exact logical slot,
item ID and tracking token. Brew family 19 and restore families 23/74 come
from the local catalogue; food requires its healing tag and explicit undosed
metadata. Dose changes and moves retain role through family metadata, not
item names or remembered slots. Ordinary food and one potion may be proposed
in one tick. Prayer restoration and brew priority are configurable. Renewal
families and Disruption Shield remain documented leads, not guessed actions.

Defensives require a fresh castable=True result and known zero cooldown;
equipment match alone is insufficient. Freedom requires a confirmed stun or
bind. Predicted-hit rules additionally require a fresh same-session D1 state,
a calibrated future check window, measured damage bound and explicit eligible
defensives. Confirmed effect absences protect one-hit blockers from accidental
stacking, and Devotion requires matching observed protection. Every proposal
retains its trigger, ability/effect observations and target evidence.

Virus cleansing requires a confirmed active effect and exactly one fresh
validated P4 cleanse route with an actual entity identity and option. Emergency
exit requires a calibrated profile binding and fresh castability; the default
route is explicitly unmapped. Missing data never becomes a default teleport,
food, defensive or safe state.

D6 calls ``note_sent(intent_id, sent_ns)`` only after accepted input. Pending
lanes wait for a newer inventory/cooldown/effect observation. A changed item
slot invalidates old preconditions without claiming consumption; a display
metadata refresh does not acknowledge input. Timeout latches a producer fault
until reset. Proposals never consume inventory optimistically.

All 25 D3 tests pass, including the required survival scenarios and a golden
S0a replay from 24 unchanged low-health records in the saved P2 capture.
That replay yields 24 potion proposals with no sent input. The input SHA-256
is ``2571e1db5280fc4233945b35a71f77e1610188849fe288aa8c6955f8a2b06618``;
the output-log SHA-256 is
``8cc9273431af467afe4f8d0f828bb2b7bc279913d492cbe02ce555f5d4d28911``.
Tests can use ``RS3_LOCAL_CAPTURE_ROOT`` for ignored capture locations; without
the local file that single test skips. Raw records and proposal logs stay local.
The Windows x64 Release build and all 10 CTest suites passed, with 103 Python
